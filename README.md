
# Syeda Maryam Zahid

### PhD Candidate | Computational Genomics & Bioinformatics

I am a PhD candidate in Agricultural Biotechnology at Erciyes University, Türkiye. My research focuses on plant genomics, disease-resistance genetics, genomic variant analysis, and computational biology.

## Research Expertise

- Whole-genome resequencing and SNP/INDEL analysis
- Candidate-region GWAS, population structure, and kinship analysis
- Plant NLR resistance-gene identification and characterization
- Molecular phylogenetics and viral phylogeography
- Reproducible bioinformatics analysis using Python and Linux

## Technical Skills

**Programming:** Python, Linux/Unix command line

**Bioinformatics:** VCF processing, GEMMA, PLINK, HMMER, MAFFT, IQ-TREE, BEAST

**Research:** GWAS, genome annotation, variant filtering, sequence analysis, phylogenetics, statistical visualization

## Selected Research Projects

**Apricot PPV Resistance Genomics** — Whole-genome resequencing, candidate-region GWAS, SNP/INDEL analysis, and molecular marker evaluation.

**Apple NLR Gene Family Analysis** — Genome-wide identification and characterization of 713 NLR genes in *Malus domestica*.

**Plum Pox Virus Phylogeography** — Phylogenetic and evolutionary analysis of viral sequence datasets to investigate genetic diversity and geographic connectivity.
